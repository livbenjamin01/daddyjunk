About Daddy Junk

At Daddy Junk, we specialize in tackling the toughest cleanout projects. For more than five years, our team has helped homeowners, families, real estate professionals, estate attorneys, and contractors restore properties through professional cleanout services.

We handle everything from estate cleanouts and extreme hoarding situations to foreclosure and eviction cleanouts. Our experienced crews remove unwanted items quickly and safely, leaving properties ready for sale, renovation, or occupancy.

Daddy Junk also assists with properties affected by mold, animal contamination, and other hazardous conditions — removing mold, contaminated contents, debris, flooring, drywall, insulation, and other affected materials.

No job is too large or too challenging. Whether you're dealing with a single-room cleanup or an entire house filled from floor to ceiling, Daddy Junk has the experience, equipment, and dedication to get the job done right.
